## Hand over to a person
A **transfer** tool passes the call to a phone number.

1. Open **Tools & Integrations** and add a transfer tool.
2. Enter the name, a description of when to use it, and the number to transfer to.
3. Attach the tool to the agent and use the **Hand to a human** step in the [Call flow editor](/agents/call-flow-editor/).
4. Go live again.

When the tool runs, the caller hears "One moment, I'll connect you now." and the call rings your number for up to 30 seconds.

## Call me
**Call me** rings your own phone with your live agent. See [Test your agent](/agents/test-your-agent/).

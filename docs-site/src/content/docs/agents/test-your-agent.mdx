## Practice calls (Talk)
Press **Talk** on an agent to speak to it from your browser. Practice calls:
- run on the Test copy and never reach real people;
- need your microphone;
- list under **Conversations**, in practice calls.

Practice calls are charged at the same per-minute rate as real calls when your workspace is billed for them. See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works/).

## Try a call (typed chat)
In the [Call flow editor](/agents/call-flow-editor/), **Try a call** lets you chat in text with your draft. It uses your real instructions, tools and knowledge.

## Call me
Once an agent is live and you have a number, **Call me** rings your own phone with the live agent, so you can hear it on a real line. It comes from one of your numbers, uses the usual per-minute price and is limited to 5 per hour. You need: a live agent, at least one number, balance, and the agent's limits not reached. Enter your number with its country code, like +919876543210.

## Testing Lab
The **Testing Lab** has practice callers (for example an impatient one) who talk to your agent and score the result. Save test cases to re-check your agent after changes. See [Help](/help/faq/).

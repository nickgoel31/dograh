A template gives your agent a ready-made job description and tone. You can change everything afterwards. Pick one when you press **New agent**.

| Template | What the agent does | Starting tone | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Front Desk** | Answer calls, answer common questions and take messages. | Friendly | A business that wants every call answered and messages taken. |
| **Bookings** | Book, move and cancel appointments. | Professional | Any business that runs on appointments. |
| **Sales Qualifier** | Qualify new leads by asking about need, budget and timing. | Playful | Sales teams that want to know which new leads are serious. |
| **Support Hero** | Help customers solve problems and escalate to a person when needed. | Friendly | Customer support lines with common, repeatable problems. |
| **Order Taker** | Take orders accurately and confirm the total. | Playful | Takeaway, food and shop orders by phone. |
| **Survey Bot** | Run a short customer survey, one question at a time. | Professional | Short customer feedback surveys. |
| **Payment Reminder** | Politely remind customers about pending payments and offer ways to pay. | Professional | Gentle reminders about pending payments. |
| **Salon Concierge** | Book salon and spa appointments, explain services and prices, and reschedule politely. | Friendly | Salons and spas. |
| **Property Assistant** | Answer property enquiries, ask about budget, location and timing, and book site visits. | Professional | Property agents and builders. |
| **Restaurant Host** | Take table reservations, answer questions about the menu and opening hours, and note special requests. | Friendly | Restaurants and cafes. |
| **Admissions Counsellor** | Answer course questions, explain fees and batches, and book a counselling call. | Friendly | Coaching centres, schools and course sellers. |
| **Hotel Reservations** | Answer room and rate questions, check what guests need, and take booking enquiries. | Professional | Hotels and guest houses. |
| **Clinic Assistant** | Help patients with appointments and clinic information, never giving medical advice. | Friendly | Clinics and doctors’ offices (the agent never gives medical advice). |

You can also start from scratch and write your own instructions.

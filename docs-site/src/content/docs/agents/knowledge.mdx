**Knowledge** is what your agent looks up while talking.

<Shot name="knowledge" alt="Knowledge page" caption="The Knowledge page." />

## Add knowledge
1. Open **Knowledge**.
2. Choose how to add:
   - **Website:** the address of a page. It is re-read automatically about every 24 hours by default.
   - **PDF** (or a text-like file): upload it from your browser.
   - **Text:** paste it in.
   - **Q&A:** type a question and its answer. These are stored as "QA - ..." entries.
3. Give each a clear, unique name. Names must be unique in your workspace; you will see a message if one is taken.

## Attach it to an agent
Open the agent and, on its **Knowledge** tab, tick the sources it may use. This saves as you tick.

## Test a question
Use **Test a question** on the Knowledge page. It shows the best matching passage and its source. It does not write out a full answer, so use it to check that the right text is found.

## Test and Live
Knowledge exists in both Test and Live. When you add a source, a twin is made for Live. When you go live, the agent's links are carried over by name.

## Unanswered questions
The **unanswered questions** report lists questions callers asked that your knowledge could not answer. To close a gap, add the answer as Q&A or text, attach it, and try the question again.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| Test a question finds nothing | Add the information, or word the source the way callers ask. |
| A source is missing in Live | Go live again so links are carried over. |
| Knowledge added before you moved to the new app is missing | Sources that existed earlier stay on the agent's Live copy but are not listed on the new Knowledge page. Add them again. |

**Goal:** a draft agent with a job, a tone and your business details.

## Before you start
- Your workspace role must be **Developer** or above ([roles](/team/roles-and-permissions/)).
- Know your business name and what the agent should do.

## Steps
1. Open **Agents** and press **New agent**.
2. Choose a [template](/agents/templates/), or start from scratch.
3. Enter the agent's name, your business name and a sentence about what you do.
4. Choose a tone (for example Friendly, Professional or Playful).
5. Press **Create**.

## What you should see
The agent opens on its page with the status **draft**. It exists only on the Test side until you [go live](/agents/versions-and-publishing/).

## If something goes wrong
- *The agent cannot go live or take practice calls and says "calls aren't set up".* Your host has not finished setting up voices and AI models. Contact support ([Contact](/help/contact-support/)).

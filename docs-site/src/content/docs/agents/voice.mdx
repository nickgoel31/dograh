The **Voice** tab lists voices you can give your agent. You can also browse them all under **Voices** in the main menu.

<Shot name="voices" alt="Voices page with search and filters" caption="The Voices page." />

## Choose a voice
1. Open the agent's **Voice** tab.
2. Search by name. Filter by language or gender.
3. Press play to hear a sample.
4. Select the voice you want. Mark favourites with the star to find them quickly.

## Voice engines
Voices come from one or more engines, such as **Studio** voices and **Google** voices. Which engines are offered depends on your host.

- **Studio voices** support the full set of [Human feel](/agents/human-feel/) settings and the [Brain](/agents/brain/) levels.
- **Google voices** use one fixed AI model, so the Fast / Balanced / Smartest levels do not apply to them.

## Readiness
Each voice shows whether it is ready to use. A voice is ready when its engine is set up and has a working key. If a voice is not ready, your agent cannot go live with it. Choose another voice or ask your host to finish the setup.

:::note
Gender, age and accent are shown only when the voice has that information. Empty fields are hidden rather than guessed.
:::

**Voices** in the main menu is the full library. Pick the voice for an agent on its [Voice tab](/agents/voice/).

<Shot name="voices" alt="Voices page with search and filters" caption="Search and filter the voice library." />

- **Search** by name.
- **Filter** by language and gender. Gender, age and accent appear only where the voice has that information; empty fields are hidden, not guessed.
- **Play** a sample to hear it. Samples are made on demand and cached.
- **Favourite** voices with the star. Favourites are per person.
- Which engines you see depends on your host (Studio and Google voices by default).

## Opening hours
Each number can have opening hours. Outside them, callers hear your message instead of the agent. The default is: "We're closed right now. Please call back during our opening hours."

1. Open **Phone Numbers** and choose the number.
2. Turn on business hours and pick a time zone.
3. Add one or more opening times. Each has days, a start and an end such as 09:00 to 18:00. The end must be after the start.
4. Optionally write your own closed message (up to 300 characters).

Turning hours on needs at least one opening time: "Add at least one opening time, or turn business hours off."

## Paused agents and limits
If the agent is paused, callers hear: "Sorry, we can't take your call right now. Please try again later." The same applies when you reach your monthly spend limit.

## Fallback number
A fallback number can be stored on a number for sending callers to a person. Use the [transfer tool](/agents/handover/) to hand a call to a person during a call.

## Reputation
The reputation field shows **Not monitored yet**. No spam-score source is connected.

## Caller ID and compliance
Swarvo AI does not make claims about telephone regulations. If you call people in India, check the current rules of TRAI and your carrier (including registration of sender names and message wording for texts) with your carrier or adviser. Text messages after a call go through the host's Plivo account and need wording registered in India (DLT).

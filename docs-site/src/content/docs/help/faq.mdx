## Getting started
**Do I need to know how to code?** No.

<Shot name="help" alt="Help page with articles" caption="The in-app Help page." />

**What is the difference between Test and Live?** Test is a free private copy for building and trying. Live is what callers reach and what uses your balance.

**Are practice calls real?** They never reach real people.

**How do I put my agent live?** Open the agent's **Publish** tab and press **Go live**.

**Can I use my own business name?** Yes, in Personality and in [branding](/team/branding-and-sub-accounts/).

**How many templates are there?** [13](/agents/templates/).

**What does Simple vs Advanced view change?** What you see. See [Simple vs Advanced](/start/simple-vs-advanced/).

## Agents
**Can my agent speak more than one language?** Up to 6. See [Languages](/agents/languages/).

**Can I write my own instructions?** Yes, in Advanced view.

**What are Fast, Balanced and Smartest?** [Brain](/agents/brain/) levels.

**Can I choose an exact model?** Yes, in Advanced view.

**How do I stop the agent cutting people off?** Set patience to **Patient** in [Human feel](/agents/human-feel/).

**How long can a call last?** 1 to 60 minutes, default 15.

**Can my agent transfer to a person?** Yes, with a [transfer tool](/agents/handover/).

**Can I limit calls per day?** Yes, under [Behavior and limits](/agents/behavior-and-limits/).

**Can I roll back a change?** Yes, restore an older [version](/agents/versions-and-publishing/).

**How do I teach it about my business?** [Knowledge](/agents/knowledge/).

**How do I see what callers asked that it could not answer?** The unanswered questions report in Knowledge.

## Phone numbers
**Can I keep my number?** Yes, [bring your own number](/numbers/bring-your-own-number/).

**Which carriers work?** Twilio, Plivo, Vobiz, Telnyx, Vonage, Cloudonix and Asterisk.

**Do I have to set the incoming-call address?** For Twilio and Plivo we do it. For the others you do.

**Can I set opening hours?** Yes, per number.

**Is number reputation monitored?** Not yet.

**Why does my number need documents?** Some numbers, especially in India, need identity documents before they are switched on.

## Conversations
**How do I find a call?** Use filters and search in [Conversations](/conversations/reading-a-call/).

**Can I download calls?** Yes, as a CSV file.

**What is "mood"?** A 1 to 5 score written from the transcript.

**What counts as a successful call?** Booked, resolved, qualified or transferred.

## Billing
**How are calls billed?** Per minute, rounded up.

**Is a call that does not connect charged?** No.

**Does the price change?** It falls as you use more minutes in a month: [Volume pricing](/billing/volume-pricing/).

**Is GST included?** Prices are shown without tax; GST is added on invoices.

**What if my balance runs out?** Live calls are refused until you add money.

**Is the card charged automatically?** No. Auto top-up emails you a payment link; nothing is charged until you pay.

## Team and security
**Which roles exist?** Owner, admin, developer, viewer.

**Can I hide recordings?** Yes, in [Privacy](/team/privacy/).

**Is there two-step sign-in?** Yes, with an authenticator app. There are no recovery codes yet.

**Can I restrict sign-in by network?** Yes, [allowed networks](/team/security/).

**Does company sign-in manage my users?** No. It only lets people with your email domain sign in with Google.

## Developers
**Can I start calls with the API?** Not with the public API today. See [API trigger](/developers/api-trigger/).

**How many requests can I make?** 120 per minute per key.

**How are webhooks signed?** HMAC-SHA256. See [Webhooks](/developers/webhooks/).

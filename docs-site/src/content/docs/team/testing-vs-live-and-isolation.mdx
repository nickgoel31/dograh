---
title: "Testing vs live data and tenant isolation"
description: "How each workspace's data is kept separate, in plain words, and its known limits."
sidebar:
  order: 6
audience: admin
readingTime: "4 min"
---

- Every workspace is two separate organizations on the platform: Test and Live.
- Every question the system asks of its database is filtered to your workspace and to Test or Live. A workspace you are not in answers "not found".
- Public ids are opaque and tamper-evident.
- Roles are checked on the server for every action.
- Test calls run only in your browser.

:::caution
Nothing here has had an outside security review or penetration test. Known gaps: the widget's website check relies on the visitor's browser; two-step sign-in has no recovery codes; webhook secrets are stored in plain text because they are needed to sign messages.
:::

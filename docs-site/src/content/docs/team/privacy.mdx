Open **Team & Settings → Privacy**.

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Hide recordings | Recordings are not offered for playback. Capture itself is a platform setting. |
| Hide personal details | Emails, phone numbers and card-like numbers become `[email]`, `[phone]`, `[card]` in transcripts and lists, and are masked in the API. |
| Consent announcement | A short notice is spoken first on every Live call. It is added to the greeting when an agent goes live. |
| Retention | Recordings and transcripts older than the period are cleared by a scheduled job. |

:::note
Masking applies to what is shown and sent from now on. Existing stored transcripts are only changed by the retention purge.
:::

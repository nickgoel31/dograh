## Invite
1. Open **Team & Settings**.
2. Press **Invite**, enter an email and choose **admin**, **developer** or **viewer**.
3. Send the invite link to the person yourself, or by email if your host has email set up. Invites last 7 days and work once.

## Accept
The invited person opens the link and signs in with **the same email** that was invited. Otherwise they see "This invite was sent to … Sign in with that email to accept it."

## Change or remove
Pick a member to change their role or remove them. The owner's role, and your own, cannot be changed or removed there.

You need the **team.manage** permission: owners and admins. See [Roles and permissions](/team/roles-and-permissions/).

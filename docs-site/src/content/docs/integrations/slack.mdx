Post a summary of every call to a Slack channel.

## Before you start
- A Slack account.
- Uses an Incoming Webhook address from Slack. Treat it like a password.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Incoming webhook address | Yes | Required | Slack → Apps → Incoming Webhooks. Start with https:// |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **Slack**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with Slack before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on Slack.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
Nothing during the call. This connection works after the call ends.

## What gets sent after each call
Swarvo AI sends a short summary of the call to your channel.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Slack did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from Slack. Check for stray spaces. |
| “Slack said no (…)” followed by a number | Slack refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

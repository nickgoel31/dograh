Add a row to a Google Sheet for every call.

## Before you start
- A Google Sheets account.
- You must share the sheet with the Google address Swarvo AI shows you, as an editor.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheet link | No | Required | Paste the sheet's link. Share it with our address below as an editor. |
| Tab name | No | Optional (default: Sheet1) | — |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **Google Sheets**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with Google Sheets before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on Google Sheets.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
Nothing during the call. This connection works after the call ends.

## What gets sent after each call
Swarvo AI sends one row with the time, caller name, phone number, mood, summary and a link to the call.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Google Sheets did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from Google Sheets. Check for stray spaces. |
| “Google Sheets said no (…)” followed by a number | Google Sheets refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

:::caution
You must share the sheet with the Google address Swarvo AI shows you, as an editor.
:::

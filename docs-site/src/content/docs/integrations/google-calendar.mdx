Check which times are free and book appointments straight into a Google Calendar.

## Before you start
- A Google Calendar account.
- You must share the calendar with the Google address Swarvo AI shows you, with permission to “Make changes to events”.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calendar ID | No | Required | Google Calendar → Settings → your calendar → Integrate → Calendar ID. Share the calendar with our address below first. |
| Time zone | No | Optional (default: Asia/Kolkata) | — |
| Opening hour (0-23) | No | Optional (default: 9) | — |
| Closing hour (0-23) | No | Optional (default: 18) | — |
| Appointment length (minutes) | No | Optional (default: 30) | — |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **Google Calendar**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with Google Calendar before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on Google Calendar.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
- **Check free appointment times**: Check which appointment times are free on a given day. Use before offering times to the caller.
- **Book an appointment**: Book an appointment once the caller has agreed a time. Only use a time that was offered as free.

## What gets sent after each call
Nothing is sent automatically after a call.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Google Calendar did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from Google Calendar. Check for stray spaces. |
| “Google Calendar said no (…)” followed by a number | Google Calendar refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

:::caution

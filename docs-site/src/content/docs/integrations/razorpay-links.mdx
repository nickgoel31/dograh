Let the agent text a payment link during a call, using your own Razorpay account.

## Before you start
- A Razorpay payment links account.
- Uses your own Razorpay account, so payments go to you, not to Swarvo AI.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Key ID | No | Required | Razorpay dashboard → API keys. |
| Key secret | Yes | Required | — |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **Razorpay payment links**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with Razorpay payment links before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on Razorpay payment links.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
- **Send a payment link**: Text the caller a payment link once they agree to pay.

## What gets sent after each call
Nothing is sent automatically after a call.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Razorpay payment links did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from Razorpay payment links. Check for stray spaces. |
| “Razorpay payment links said no (…)” followed by a number | Razorpay payment links refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

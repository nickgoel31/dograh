Add every caller to HubSpot as a contact, with a note of what happened.

## Before you start
- A HubSpot account.
- The private app must allow contacts and notes.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private app token | Yes | Required | HubSpot → Settings → Integrations → Private Apps. Allow contacts and notes. |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **HubSpot**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with HubSpot before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on HubSpot.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
Nothing during the call. This connection works after the call ends.

## What gets sent after each call
Swarvo AI sends a contact for the caller and a note describing the call.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “HubSpot did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from HubSpot. Check for stray spaces. |
| “HubSpot said no (…)” followed by a number | HubSpot refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

:::caution
The private app must allow contacts and notes.
:::

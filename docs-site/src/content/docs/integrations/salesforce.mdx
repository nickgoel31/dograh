Create a Lead in Salesforce after each call.

## Before you start
- A Salesforce account.
- Needs a Connected App with client credentials turned on.
- Workspace role of **Developer** or above.

## What you will enter
| Field | Secret? | Required? | Hint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your Salesforce address | No | Required | Like https://yourcompany.my.salesforce.com |
| Consumer key | Yes | Required | — |
| Consumer secret | Yes | Required | From a Connected App with client credentials turned on. |

Secret fields are hidden after you save them and never shown again.

## Steps
1. Open **Integrations** (or **Tools**) and choose **Salesforce**.
2. Fill in the fields above.
3. Press **Connect**. We check the details with Salesforce before saving. If they are wrong you will see a plain message; fix the field and try again.
4. Open your agent, go to its tools, and switch on Salesforce.
5. Go live again so the live copy of the agent gets the connection.

## What the agent can do on a call
Nothing during the call. This connection works after the call ends.

## What gets sent after each call
Swarvo AI sends a new Lead for the caller.

## If something goes wrong
| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| “Salesforce did not accept those details. Check them and try again.” | Copy the values again from Salesforce. Check for stray spaces. |
| “Salesforce said no (…)” followed by a number | Salesforce refused the request. Check the permissions you gave the token or app. |
| It connects but nothing arrives | Make sure the agent has the connection switched on and that you pressed **Go live** afterwards. |

:::caution
Needs a Connected App with client credentials turned on.
:::

- Your **wallet** holds prepaid money in rupees. Live calls take money from it.
- Your host may give new workspaces **free minutes** once. They are used first.
- Calls are billed **per minute, rounded up**. A 61-second call is 2 minutes. A call that never connects is free.
- **Practice calls** are charged at the same rate when billed.
- **Phone number rent** is billed separately.
- The price per minute falls as you use more minutes in a month: [Volume pricing](/billing/volume-pricing/).
- Your wallet balance and minutes left show at the top of the page.

## Spend limit
Set a monthly spend limit in Billing. At 80% you see a warning. At 100% new calls are refused with a polite message.

## Where the money goes
Billing shows the split between phone, voice, speech to text and AI brain.

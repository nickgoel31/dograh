The price per minute depends on how many minutes your workspace has used **this month**. Each call minute is charged at the rate for the band your usage is in **when the call is billed** (the rate is refreshed from your month's total about once a minute). Prices are in Indian rupees (₹) and are shown without tax.

| Minutes used this month | Price per minute |
|---|---|
| 1 to 1,000 | ₹6.75 |
| 1,001 to 5,000 | ₹6.00 |
| 5,001 to 15,000 | ₹5.50 |
| 15,001 to 25,000 | ₹5.00 |
| 25,001 to 37,500 | ₹4.60 |
| 37,501 to 50,000 | ₹4.20 |
| 50,001 to 75,000 | ₹3.80 |
| 75,001 to 1,00,000 | ₹3.40 |
| Above 1,00,000 | Contact sales (the last rate, ₹3.40, applies meanwhile) |

:::note
These are the default prices. The person hosting Swarvo AI can change the bands. The Billing page in your app always shows the rates that apply to you.
:::

## Where each minute's price goes
Of every minute's price, ₹0.40 goes to the phone call itself. The rest is split into **voice 60%**, **speech to text 30%** and **AI brain 10%**. Billing shows this split so you can see where your money goes.

## Worked example
Your workspace has used 990 minutes this month, so your rate is ₹6.75. A caller talks for 5 minutes and 10 seconds. That call is billed as 6 minutes (see [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works/)), so it costs 6 x ₹6.75 = ₹40.50. Your rate is recalculated from the month's total about once a minute, so after you pass 1,000 minutes the next calls are charged at ₹6.00. The rate never goes back up in the same month, because your total only grows.

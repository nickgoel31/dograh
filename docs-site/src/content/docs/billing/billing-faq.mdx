**What happens at zero balance?** Live calls are refused until you add money.

**A payment failed.** Try again or use another method. If money left your account but your balance did not change, wait a few minutes (a back-up confirmation also runs) and then contact support.

**Can I get a refund?** Refund terms are set by your host. Contact support.

**Why was I charged for a short call?** Calls round up to the next whole minute.

**Why was a call free?** A call that never connected is free.

**Are practice calls free?** They run on Test and are billed at the same rate when your workspace is billed for them.

**Is rent included in the price per minute?** No. Number rent is billed separately.

- Calls are billed **per minute, rounded up**.
- A call that never connects is **free**.
- Practice calls are charged at the same rate when billed.
- Number rent is billed separately.

| Call length | Billed minutes |
|---|---|
| 0 seconds, never connected | 0 |
| 30 seconds | 1 |
| 60 seconds | 1 |
| 61 seconds | 2 |
| 5 minutes 10 seconds | 6 |

At the default first-band price of ₹6.75 per minute, a 61-second call costs 2 x ₹6.75 = ₹13.50. See [Volume pricing](/billing/volume-pricing/).

The call detail page splits the cost into phone, voice, speech to text and AI brain.

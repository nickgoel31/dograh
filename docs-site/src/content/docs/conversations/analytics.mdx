**Analytics** is computed from your calls. Live and Test are separate.

<Shot name="analytics" alt="Analytics page with call, quality and cost charts" caption="The Analytics page." />

| Section | Shows |
|---|---|
| Business | Calls, bookings, leads qualified, hours of work replaced, estimated money saved |
| Quality | Share of calls that went well, average reply speed, funnel through the flow |
| Cost | Total, per call, per minute and a per-day split |

"Money saved" is minutes multiplied by an hourly figure for a person's cost that **you can edit**. It is an assumption, not a fact.

**Not tracked yet:** interruption rate and caller mood trend. The page says so instead of showing invented numbers.

**Compare** shows the previous period of equal length. Each view reads up to 20,000 calls.

## Weekly report
A weekly report is emailed by the scheduled job if your host has set up email. See [Alerts and emailed summaries](/conversations/alerts-and-summaries/).

Alerts appear in the **bell** in the top bar. The owner is also emailed once when:
- a call fails (the last 36 hours are checked), or
- the balance is low.

Other alerts: 80% of the monthly spend limit used, the spend limit reached, and a webhook that stopped working after 6 tries.

Summaries and the weekly report are emailed when the host's email is configured. Without email, nothing is sent and links are shown inside the app instead.

**Campaigns** call a list of people with an agent. They run only in **Live** mode.

<Shot name="campaigns" alt="Campaigns page" caption="The Campaigns page." />

## Before you start
- A live agent and a number to call from.
- A CSV with a phone column (names such as phone, mobile, number, contact, cell or whatsapp are recognised) and optional extra columns.
- Permission to call these people.

## Steps
1. Open **Campaigns** and press **New campaign**.
2. Upload the CSV (up to 5 MB and 10,000 contacts). Bad rows and duplicates are reported.
3. Pick the agent.
4. Confirm that you have permission to call these people and that you checked the Do Not Call list. This is logged.
5. Start.

## Rules the system applies
- Numbers must be in international format (for example +919876543210).
- Calls go out only between 09:00 and 21:00 in the contact's local time (host-adjustable).
- Numbers on the [do-not-call list](/conversations/contacts-and-do-not-call/) are skipped.
- Every create, start, pause and resume is recorded.

## Pacing and retries
The platform dials on its own schedule with its own retry settings. You can pause and resume at any time.

:::caution
There is no live registry check. You attest that you may call these people.
:::

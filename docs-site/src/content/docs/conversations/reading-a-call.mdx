## The list
Each row shows when the call happened, who called, the agent, the length, the outcome and the cost. Use filters (such as outcome) and the search box to find calls, including words spoken inside transcripts. You can export calls as a CSV file.

:::note
Outcome and search filters look through the newest 2,000 calls. If your history is longer, the page tells you the results were limited.
:::

## Outcomes
A call counts as going well when it is **booked**, **resolved**, **qualified** or **transferred**. Other outcomes include voicemail, no answer and failed.

## The call detail page
Press a call to open it.
- **Who spoke when.** The transcript, turn by turn.
- **How fast the agent replied.** From the platform's recorded speed.
- **Summary and mood.** A one-sentence summary and a mood score (1 upset to 5 delighted), written once from the transcript.
- **What we found out.** Answers the agent collected during the call.
- **Where the money went.** The cost split.
- **Recording.** A player, if recordings are allowed in [Privacy](/team/privacy/). The link lasts one hour.

## Practice calls
Calls you make from the browser are listed separately under practice calls.

**Contacts** lists everyone your agents have talked to. It is built from your calls, and an agent can add what it learned.

## Opting out
When a caller says a "stop calling me" phrase (in English or common Hindi and Hinglish), they are recognised as opting out and added to the do-not-call list.

## Block a number
Add a number on the blocked list on the Contacts page. Remove it from the same list.

## Export
You can export contacts as a CSV file.

Campaigns skip numbers on the list. See [Campaigns](/conversations/campaigns/).

## Before you start
- A web browser and a microphone (for the practice call).
- Basic facts about your business: name, what you do, opening hours, prices you are happy for the agent to quote.
- Money in your wallet if you want to take real calls. Your host may give new workspaces some free minutes ([how billing works](/billing/how-billing-works/)).

## Steps

### 1. Sign up
Open the app and create an account with your email and a password, or use **Sign in with Google** if the button is shown. Your first workspace is created for you. It starts in **Test** mode: free, browser-only and safe.

:::tip[Checkpoint]
You see the **Home** page with a short getting-started checklist.
:::

### 2. Create an agent from a template
1. Open **Agents** and press **New agent**.
2. Pick a [template](/agents/templates/) close to your needs, for example **Front Desk** or **Bookings**.
3. Fill in your business name and what you do. Choose a tone.

:::tip[Checkpoint]
Your agent appears in the list with the status **draft**.
:::

### 3. Pick a voice
Open the agent's **Voice** tab. Search or filter by language, and press play to hear voices. Choose one. See [Voice](/agents/voice/).

### 4. Teach it about your business
On the **Knowledge** tab, add your website, a PDF, some text or question-and-answer pairs. See [Knowledge](/agents/knowledge/).

### 5. Try a call in your browser
Press **Talk** and speak to your agent. Ask the questions your real callers ask. If an answer is wrong, fix the instructions or knowledge and talk again.

:::tip[Checkpoint]
The agent greets you with your business name and answers at least two questions correctly.
:::

### 6. Get a phone number
Open **Phone Numbers**, search for a number and buy it, or [bring your own](/numbers/bring-your-own-number/). Choose your agent as the one that answers. See [Buy a number](/numbers/buy-a-number/).

### 7. Add money
Real calls use your balance. Open **Billing & Usage** and add money. See [Adding money](/billing/adding-money/).

### 8. Go live
Open the agent's **Publish** tab. Fix anything marked as a blocker, then press **Go live**. See [Versions and publishing](/agents/versions-and-publishing/).

## What you should see
Switch to **Live** and call your number from another phone. Your agent answers. Afterwards the call appears under **Conversations**.

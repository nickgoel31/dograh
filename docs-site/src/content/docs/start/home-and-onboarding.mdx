## First-time setup (welcome wizard)
New workspaces open a short wizard called **Let's set up your first agent**.

1. **Business.** Type what your business is called, and what you do and how the agent should help callers.
2. **Say hello to your agent.** Try your new agent in the browser.
3. **Ready to take real calls?** Go on to a phone number and going live.

You can skip the wizard and create agents yourself from **Agents**.

## The Home page
<Shot name="home" alt="Home page with a getting-started checklist" caption="Home shows your first-run checklist and today's numbers." />

- **Getting-started checklist** ("Let's get you talking in 4 easy steps"). It is worked out from your real workspace, so steps tick themselves when you complete them.
- **Talk to your agent now.** A quick way to try an agent.
- **Numbers.** Calls today, success rate, minutes used and spend. A call counts as successful when it is **booked**, **resolved**, **qualified** or **transferred**.

:::note
In demo mode the app has a "Show new user view" switch. It only exists in demo mode.
:::

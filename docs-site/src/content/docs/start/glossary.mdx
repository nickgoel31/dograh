**Admin.** A role that can do everything except delete the workspace.

**Agent.** An AI assistant that talks on the phone.

**API key.** A secret code that lets your software talk to Swarvo AI. Keys start `sk_test_` or `sk_live_`.

**Audit log.** A record of important changes: keys, webhooks, invites, role changes, settings, two-step sign-in and going live.

**Brain.** The AI model that thinks for your agent. Levels: Fast, Balanced, Smartest.

**Call flow.** The steps an agent follows.

**Campaign.** A batch of outbound calls to a list of people.

**Consent announcement.** A short notice spoken at the start of every Live call, such as "this call may be recorded".

**Do not call.** People who must not be called. Campaigns skip them.

**Go live.** Publish your agent so callers reach the new version.

**Knowledge.** Material the agent can look things up in.

**Live.** The side of your workspace that real callers reach and that uses your balance.

**Practice call.** A call from your browser to your own agent.

**Retention.** How long recordings and transcripts are kept.

**Speech to text.** Turning the caller's voice into words.

**Test.** The private, free side of your workspace.

**Tool.** An ability the agent can use during a call.

**Wallet.** Your prepaid balance.

**Webhook.** A message Swarvo AI sends to your server when something happens, such as a call ending.

**Workspace.** Your business account.

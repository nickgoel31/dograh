## Who it is for
Any business that gets or makes phone calls: clinics, salons, restaurants, hotels, property agents, coaching centres, shops and support teams. You do not need to write code.

## A 60-second tour
1. **Agents.** Create an agent from a [template](/agents/templates/), describe your business, and choose a voice.
2. **Try it.** Press **Talk** to have a practice call from your browser. Practice calls never reach real people.
3. **Phone number.** [Buy a number](/numbers/buy-a-number/) or [bring your own](/numbers/bring-your-own-number/), and attach it to your agent.
4. **Go live.** Press **Go live**. Callers now reach your agent.
5. **Conversations.** Read [every call](/conversations/reading-a-call/): transcript, summary, mood, what the agent found out, cost and recording.

## What it can do
- Answer questions from your own documents ([Knowledge](/agents/knowledge/)).
- Book into [Google Calendar, Calendly or Cal.com](/integrations/at-a-glance/).
- Send each call to your CRM, Slack, WhatsApp or a Google Sheet.
- [Hand a call to a person](/agents/handover/) when needed.
- Call lists of people ([Campaigns](/conversations/campaigns/)).
- Sit on your website as a [call or chat button](/agents/website-widget/).

## What it costs
You pay per minute of calls, rounded up. See [How billing works](/billing/how-billing-works/) and [Volume pricing](/billing/volume-pricing/).
